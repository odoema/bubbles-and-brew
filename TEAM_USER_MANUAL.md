# Bubbles & Brew — Team User Manual

**Version:** 1.0 — Launch Preparation  
**Business promise:** **WASH, WARM AND ROLL**  
**Primary business:** Car wash and vehicle care  
**Supporting experience:** Café lounge, refreshments, beauty/personal care, memberships

---

## 1. What this system is

The Bubbles & Brew website is the customer-facing front door for the business. It is designed to help customers discover services, book a wash, understand membership plans and experience the café/beauty offering while the vehicle is being serviced.

The website is being developed as the foundation for a larger operations system. Staff should therefore treat information shown to customers as business information: prices, services, opening information and availability must be kept accurate.

## 2. Main customer menu

| Menu item | Purpose | Team responsibility |
|---|---|---|
| **Home** | Brand, promise and first impression | Keep brand information accurate |
| **Book Wash** | Customer booking journey | Monitor and action bookings |
| **Monthly Plans** | Membership plans and benefits | Explain plans and verify member benefits |
| **Cafe Lounge** | Café menu and waiting experience | Keep food/drink information and availability current |
| **Beauty Parlour** | Complementary personal-care services | Keep service list and availability current |
| **My Perks** | Customer loyalty/member benefits | Verify points, discounts and member status |

## 3. Booking workflow

1. Customer selects **Book Wash**.
2. Customer enters vehicle information.
3. Customer selects a wash service.
4. Customer enters name and mobile number.
5. Customer chooses date and preferred time.
6. Customer reviews the booking summary.
7. Customer submits the booking.
8. Staff confirm the booking through the operational process used by the business.
9. On arrival, staff verify the vehicle registration and selected service before work begins.
10. After completion, staff confirm the vehicle is ready and complete the customer handover.

> **Important:** The current website release may operate in preview/local-storage mode. Do not represent a local browser booking as a confirmed production booking until the live backend/operations connection has been verified.

## 4. Café Lounge — staff workflow

The café is a supporting part of the car-wash experience. Customers should be able to use the lounge while their vehicles are being serviced.

Staff should be able to answer:

- What drinks are available?
- What food is available?
- What is currently unavailable?
- What are the prices?
- What is the expected preparation time?
- Where should a customer wait?
- Can the café order be associated with the customer's visit?

Never promise an unavailable item. If stock changes, update the customer-facing information through the approved content-management process.

## 5. Beauty Parlour — staff workflow

Beauty services are complementary to the main vehicle-care experience. Staff should clearly communicate:

- available services;
- service prices;
- estimated service time;
- whether an appointment is required;
- current availability.

The beauty offering must never confuse customers about the primary Bubbles & Brew proposition: **the car wash remains the main business.**

## 6. Membership and My Perks

**Monthly Plans** explains the membership products. **My Perks** is the customer-benefit destination.

Staff should distinguish:

- membership subscription;
- included washes;
- member discounts;
- loyalty points;
- complimentary café benefits where applicable;
- priority booking;
- fleet/corporate arrangements.

Do not manually promise a benefit that is not visible in the approved membership rules.

## 7. Customer service standard

Use the Bubbles & Brew service sequence:

**WELCOME → CONFIRM → CARE → UPDATE → COMPLETE → THANK**

- **WELCOME:** acknowledge the customer promptly.
- **CONFIRM:** confirm vehicle, registration and requested service.
- **CARE:** handle the vehicle and customer property responsibly.
- **UPDATE:** communicate delays or changes early.
- **COMPLETE:** perform the final quality check before handover.
- **THANK:** close the visit professionally and invite the customer back.

## 8. Data and privacy

Only collect information required for the service. Do not share customer phone numbers, vehicle information, bookings or membership information outside authorised business processes.

Do not store passwords in notebooks, WhatsApp messages or spreadsheets unless an approved process specifically requires it.

## 9. When something goes wrong

### Booking problem
Record the customer's name, mobile number, vehicle registration, requested service and intended date/time. Escalate rather than asking the customer to repeatedly submit the same form.

### Website problem
Capture the page, button, device and exact error message. Take a screenshot where possible. Do not modify production code yourself unless you are authorised to do so.

### Wrong price/service information
Stop quoting the disputed price and escalate to the responsible manager for confirmation.

### Customer complaint
Listen first, confirm the facts, avoid arguing, document the issue and escalate according to management procedure.
