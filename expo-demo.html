Walk-in demonstration

On the dashboard click + Walk-in booking. Enter a customer, vehicle, registration, service, date and time. The walk-in appears as Checked in because the customer is already physically at the wash.

WALK-IN→CHECKED IN→WASHING→READY→COMPLETED

What to say to a visitor

30-second pitch

“Welcome to Bubbles & Brew — Wash, Warm and Roll. We are turning a traditional car-wash visit into a connected customer experience. A customer can discover services and book digitally, while the business can manage the vehicle journey from request to completion. The café and complementary services turn waiting time into part of the experience. This MVP proves the customer and operations workflow; the next layer adds secure staff accounts, payments, notifications, loyalty and real-time multi-device operations.”
