WASH, WARM AND ROLL

The quick operating guide for the customer-facing website and the service experience behind it.

1. What the app does

Bubbles & Brew is a car-wash-first customer experience. The website helps customers discover vehicle-care services, book a wash, understand monthly plans, access Café Lounge information, explore Beauty Parlour services and understand My Perks.

The car wash is the main business. Café and beauty services make the customer's waiting time more valuable.

3. Booking workflow

  1. Select Book Wash.
  2. Enter vehicle make/model and registration.
  3. Select vehicle type and wash service.
  4. Enter customer name and mobile number.
  5. Select date and preferred time.
  6. Review the summary.
  7. Submit the booking.
  8. Staff confirm and action the request through the approved operations process.
  9. Verify vehicle registration and service at arrival.
  10. Complete the quality check and handover.
If the website is still in preview/local-storage mode, do not describe the browser submission as a confirmed live booking.

4. Café Lounge

The Café Lounge exists to make the wash visit better. Staff should know the current drinks, refreshments, bites, prices, preparation times and unavailable items.

Never promise an item that is unavailable. When menu information changes, use the approved content update process.

5. Beauty Parlour

Beauty is complementary to vehicle care. Staff should clearly communicate the available services, prices, expected duration and appointment requirements. Do not let the supporting service obscure the main Bubbles & Brew proposition.

6. Monthly Plans vs My Perks

Monthly Plans explains what a customer can subscribe to. My Perks explains the benefits a customer receives through membership and loyalty.

  • Included washes
  • Member discounts
  • Priority booking
  • Loyalty points
  • Approved café benefits
  • Fleet/corporate arrangements

7. Service standard

WELCOME → CONFIRM → CARE → UPDATE → COMPLETE → THANK

  • Welcome: acknowledge customers promptly.
  • Confirm: verify vehicle, registration and requested service.
  • Care: protect the vehicle and customer property.
  • Update: communicate delays early.
  • Complete: quality-check before handover.
  • Thank: close professionally and invite the customer back.

8. Daily opening checklist

  • Website opens correctly.
  • Main menu links work.
  • Book Wash opens.
  • Wash prices are correct.
  • Café information is current.
  • Beauty information is current.
  • Membership information is current.
  • Today's operational capacity is known.
  • Customer enquiries are monitored.

9. Daily closing checklist

  • All customer vehicles handed over or carried forward.
  • Outstanding bookings identified.
  • Complaints recorded.
  • Stock/service issues reported.
  • Website errors documented.
  • Customer information is not left exposed.

10. When something goes wrong

  • Booking issue: record customer name, phone, registration, service and intended date/time, then escalate.
  • Website issue: capture the page, button, device and exact error; take a screenshot.
  • Wrong price: stop quoting the disputed amount until management confirms it.
  • Complaint: listen, confirm facts, document and escalate; never argue.

Golden rule

The website is part of the service. If a customer can see it, the team should be prepared to deliver it — or clearly explain that the item is temporarily unavailable.
